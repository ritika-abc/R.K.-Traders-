About R.K. Traders

Your Trusted Partner for Industrial Products

Established in 2008, R.K. Traders is a trusted supplier based in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, offering quality lubricants, waterproofing chemicals, adhesives and abrasives for a wide range of industrial, construction and commercial applications.

With a customer-focused approach and extensive industry experience, we serve companies, factories, contractors and businesses across India. Our commitment is to provide reliable products, professional service and long-term value to every customer we work with.
